2 Chief Complaints

In patient's own words, in chronological order. Mention duration. Is it an aggravation of baseline chronic symptoms? (Boloor: e.g., COPD patient with increased dyspnea for 3 days)

Tip: Write like — “Fever for 3 days”, “Chest pain on exertion for 1 week”, “Shortness of breath NYHA III for 2 months, worsened for 3 days”
